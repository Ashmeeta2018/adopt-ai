The question usually comes in a follow-up email after the first proposal.

"The build price makes sense. But $149 a month for ongoing support seems... low. What's the catch?"

There's no catch. Here's what $149 a month is actually buying.

## What the Operate fee covers

**Monitoring.** We watch the three key metrics — volume, escalation rate, error rate — on a cadence that scales from twice-daily in the first month to weekly after the agent is stable. This isn't a dashboard you log into. It's us looking at numbers and flagging when something changes.

**Incident response.** If the agent breaks — and at some point, it will — we fix it. The ops engineer who built it is the one who responds. Average resolution time on our incidents is 47 minutes during business hours.

**One tuning cycle per quarter.** Production data drifts. We run one retraining cycle every three months using the accumulated escalation data as new labels. We don't charge extra for this.

**A monthly written report.** One page. Volume, escalation rate, incidents, recommendations. Plain English.

That's it. No strategy calls. No roadmap reviews. No account management.

## Why it's priced at $149

Bluntly: the Operate phase is not where our margin is.

A Spark build takes two weeks of real engineering time. A Lift takes five or six. That's where we do the work. The operate phase is mostly monitoring, and monitoring at scale is cheap when the infrastructure is good.

What we're really paying for in the operate period is the relationship. We stay close to the agent. We see the data. When something changes — and it always does, eventually — we're already looking at it.

If we priced operate at $499 a month, clients would cancel when the agent had been running quietly for six months. Then something would change, and no one would be watching. That's a worse outcome for everyone.

We'd rather have the agent running under our operate umbrella for two years at $149 than have it go dark after six months because the fee felt unjustifiable.

## What you're not getting

Some things aren't in the $149:

**New workflows.** If you want to add a second agent, that's a new Spark or Lift engagement. The operate fee is for the agent we built, not for a roadmap.

**Custom reporting.** The monthly written report is standard. If you need custom dashboards or BI integrations, that's out of scope.

**Major model upgrades.** If the underlying model we used gets deprecated and needs replacing, we'll quote that separately. In practice, we've had to do this once in eighteen months.

**24/7 SLA with sub-15-minute response.** Our standard operate response is four hours after business hours. If you need tighter than that, we can discuss an enterprise operate tier.

## The real reason clients stay on Operate

It's not the monitoring. It's that we're still there.

When the ops lead changes jobs — and it happens — the new person inherits a system with twelve months of documented incidents, a plain-English runbook, and an engineer who knows the history. That handoff is worth more than the $149.
